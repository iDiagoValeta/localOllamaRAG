## 1. Methodology

RAGAS runs on the checkpoints already generated by the BM25 reinference step. It does not regenerate answers: it reuses the stored question, retrieved contexts, generated answer and ground truth.

The paired comparisons use the same questions and generator in both variants:

| Pipeline component | `baseline_all_on` | `all_off` |
| --- | :---: | :---: |
| `USAR_BUSQUEDA_HIBRIDA` | enabled | disabled |
| `USAR_LLM_QUERY_DECOMPOSITION` | enabled | disabled |
| `USAR_RERANKER` | enabled | disabled |
| `EXPANDIR_CONTEXTO` | enabled | disabled |
| `USAR_OPTIMIZACION_CONTEXTO` | enabled | disabled |
| `USAR_RECOMP_SYNTHESIS` | enabled | disabled |

`all_off` is not a no-retrieval baseline. It keeps the basic vector-semantic retrieval path plus generation. The delta therefore measures the incremental value of the advanced retrieval, reranking, context-processing and synthesis stages over a functional vanilla RAG baseline.

Metric meanings:

- **answer_correctness**: factual correctness against the reference answer.
- **faithfulness**: consistency of the generated answer with the retrieved context.
- **answer_relevancy**: whether the answer addresses the question.
- **context_precision**: whether the relevant retrieved fragments are ranked near the top.
- **context_recall**: whether the retrieved context contains the information needed by the reference.

All values below are percentages. Delta (pp) means `baseline_all_on - all_off` in percentage points.

## 4. Paired all_on vs all_off analysis

| Set | Delta global mean | answer_correctness | faithfulness | answer_relevancy | context_precision | context_recall | Improves / tie / degrades |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| ES local | **+7.22 pp** | +0.79 | -0.73 | +6.11 | **+24.91** | +5.00 | 36 / 1 / 13 |
| CA local | **+8.21 pp** | +0.75 | -1.60 | +3.82 | **+29.10** | +9.00 | 35 / 0 / 15 |
| RagBench dev | **+6.07 pp** | +7.15 | -2.67 | +8.89 | **+11.96** | +5.00 | 38 / 0 / 12 |

The dominant effect is retrieval quality, not a small stylistic change in generated answers. `context_precision` is the largest positive delta in all three paired comparisons:

- ES local: +24.91 pp
- CA local: +29.10 pp
- RagBench dev: +11.96 pp

This is the clearest RAGAS confirmation that the BM25 rerun plus the full inference pipeline retrieves better evidence and ranks it more effectively. `context_recall` also improves in all paired sets, so the pipeline is not merely reordering fragments; it also recovers more of the necessary evidence.

`answer_correctness` improves only slightly on ES/CA (+0.79 and +0.75 pp), but improves materially on RagBench dev (+7.15 pp). This matches the nature of the datasets: local ES/CA answers were already semantically strong, while RagBench has shorter reference answers and more room for semantic alignment gains.

`faithfulness` is the only metric that drops slightly in the full pipeline across paired sets. The drop is small relative to the retrieval gains and remains at a high absolute level: 93.33% for ES, 94.75% for CA and 92.60% for RagBench dev. The likely explanation is that the richer pipeline produces more complete answers, creating more factual claims for the judge to verify. This should be described as a minor trade-off, not as a hallucination problem.

## 5. Single-variant RagBench eval and visual results

### 5.1 RagBench eval 40p

The definitive full pipeline obtains a global RAGAS mean of **81.62** over 200 questions:

| Metric | Score |
| --- | ---: |
| answer_correctness | 56.82 |
| faithfulness | 91.98 |
| answer_relevancy | 79.71 |
| context_precision | 86.53 |
| context_recall | 93.08 |
| **Global mean** | **81.62** |

This is the strongest large-scale validation set in the BM25 RAGAS pass. It combines high faithfulness with strong context recall and precision, which means the full pipeline usually retrieves the needed evidence and grounds its answers in that evidence.

The lower `answer_correctness` compared with ES/CA is expected for RagBench: references are often compact and extractive, while the RAG system tends to produce fuller explanatory answers. This depresses factual-correctness scoring compared with local curated datasets, but does not undermine the retrieval and grounding conclusion.

### 5.2 RagBench visual image/table 25p

The definitive full pipeline obtains a global RAGAS mean of **70.00** over 125 questions:

| Metric | Score |
| --- | ---: |
| answer_correctness | 46.68 |
| faithfulness | 88.52 |
| answer_relevancy | 72.47 |
| context_precision | 70.47 |
| context_recall | 71.87 |
| **Global mean** | **70.00** |

This is the hardest benchmark in the final evaluation. Its lower score is driven by visual/table questions, where retrieval must recover evidence from more complex document structures and the answer often depends on localized image or table content.

Subset aggregation is balanced:

| Source type | n | answer_correctness | faithfulness | answer_relevancy | context_precision | context_recall | Global mean |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| text-image | 104 | 46.50 | 90.24 | 73.13 | 71.35 | 68.59 | **69.96** |
| text-table | 21 | 47.58 | 79.96 | 69.18 | 66.11 | 88.10 | **70.19** |

The image subset has stronger faithfulness and precision, while the table subset has stronger recall but lower faithfulness. The global means are nearly identical, so the visual benchmark should be presented as uniformly difficult rather than as a single failing modality.

## 6. Relation to lexical and embedding metrics

The lexical/embedding report already showed that the BM25 full pipeline improves Token F1, ROUGE-L and BERTScore F1 across all five paired runs. RAGAS adds two important confirmations:

1. **The improvement is semantically meaningful.** ES, CA and RagBench dev all improve in RAGAS global mean, not just in overlap-based metrics.
2. **The main mechanism is better retrieval.** RAGAS identifies `context_precision` and `context_recall` as the most consistent improvements, which aligns with the intended BM25 + hybrid retrieval rerun.

For the three paired sets that were evaluated with RAGAS:

| Set | BERTScore F1 delta | RAGAS global mean delta | Main RAGAS driver |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| ES local | +2.92 pp | **+7.22 pp** | `context_precision` +24.91 pp |
| CA local | +5.95 pp | **+8.21 pp** | `context_precision` +29.10 pp |
| RagBench dev | +8.95 pp | **+6.07 pp** | `context_precision` +11.96 pp |

The two metric families agree on direction. RAGAS is especially useful because it explains why the full pipeline helps: it is not simply producing lexically closer answers, it is retrieving and ranking more relevant context.

## 7. Limitations

1. **RAGAS is still an LLM judge.** Scores should be treated as a semantic estimate, not as an oracle. They are strongest when triangulated with lexical metrics, BERTScore and qualitative examples.
2. **RagBench references are short and extractive.** This can lower `answer_correctness` for longer but still useful answers, especially when the answer contains extra explanatory material.
3. **`faithfulness` penalizes extra claims.** The full pipeline often produces richer answers; a small drop in faithfulness can appear even when answer relevance and context quality improve.
4. **RagBench eval and visual were not re-run for `all_off` in RAGAS.** Their RAGAS values validate the final `baseline_all_on` configuration, while the expensive paired ablation is limited to ES, CA and RagBench dev.

## 8. Conclusion for the TFG defense

The BM25 RAGAS pass confirms the core conclusion of the lexical and embedding evaluation: the complete inference pipeline is better than the semantic-only baseline.

The most defensible claim is retrieval-centric:

> The full pipeline improves the quality of the evidence retrieved for the answer. RAGAS shows large gains in `context_precision` and consistent gains in `context_recall`, while preserving high faithfulness.

The final answer-quality story is:

- Local ES and CA are strong and stable: global RAGAS mean reaches **86.70** and **85.40** with `baseline_all_on`.
- RagBench dev confirms that the gain generalizes to English text questions: **+6.07 pp** global RAGAS mean over `all_off`.
- RagBench eval validates the final full pipeline at larger scale: **81.62** global RAGAS mean across 200 questions.
- RagBench visual remains the hardest setting: **70.00** global RAGAS mean, with evidence retrieval from image/table-derived content as the main challenge.

The small `faithfulness` decrease in paired comparisons should be acknowledged as a trade-off of richer answers, but it does not overturn the conclusion because absolute faithfulness remains above 92% in all paired `baseline_all_on` runs.

## 9. Reproducibility

The final BM25 RAGAS command used the AWS provider and evaluated the completed checkpoints without overwriting the archived pre-BM25 artifacts:

```powershell
python research/evaluation/evaluate.py --provider aws --output-root "research/evaluation/runs/ragas_aws_revaluation" --ragas-max-workers 8 --ragas-batch-size 8 `
  --checkpoint "research/evaluation/runs/ragas/comparisons/bm25rerun_es/checkpoints/baseline_all_on.json" `
  --checkpoint "research/evaluation/runs/ragas/comparisons/bm25rerun_es/checkpoints/all_off.json" `
  --checkpoint "research/evaluation/runs/ragas/comparisons/bm25rerun_ca_ca/checkpoints/baseline_all_on.json" `
  --checkpoint "research/evaluation/runs/ragas/comparisons/bm25rerun_ca_ca/checkpoints/all_off.json" `
  --checkpoint "research/evaluation/runs/ragas/comparisons/bm25rerun_ragbench_dev/checkpoints/baseline_all_on.json" `
  --checkpoint "research/evaluation/runs/ragas/comparisons/bm25rerun_ragbench_dev/checkpoints/all_off.json" `
  --checkpoint "research/evaluation/runs/ragas/comparisons/bm25rerun_ragbench_eval/checkpoints/baseline_all_on.json" `
  --checkpoint "research/evaluation/runs/ragas/comparisons/bm25rerun_ragbench_visual/checkpoints/baseline_all_on.json"
```

Generated artifacts:

- `research/evaluation/runs/ragas_aws_revaluation/comparisons/<run>/<variant>/scores.csv` - per-question RAGAS scores.
- `research/evaluation/runs/ragas_aws_revaluation/comparisons/<run>/<variant>/debug.json` - per-question judge traces and metadata.
- `research/evaluation/runs/ragas_aws_revaluation/comparisons/<run>/aggregates/` - source-type aggregation where available.
